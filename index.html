Poles and zeros

Open-loop mode closes the loop with unity negative feedback, T(s) = K·G(s) / (1 + K·G(s)). Step responses are computed exactly with a zero-order-hold discretisation of the state-space model. Bode asymptotes are the textbook straight-line approximations: the magnitude slope changes by ±20 dB/decade at each real corner frequency and ±40 dB/decade at each complex pair (at ωn); the phase steps at each corner frequency, by ±90° for a real root and ±180° for a complex pair.
